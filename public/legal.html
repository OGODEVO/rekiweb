Terms & membership

Reki Web is a personal supplement routine and feeling tracker for adults. It is not medical advice or proof that a supplement caused a change. Follow your label and clinician's guidance; do not change a prescribed regimen based on a tracker comparison.

The free preview stores your record in your browser. Membership adds account-backed saving and access to that saved record when you sign in on another device using the same Whop account. Saving needs an internet connection. Clearing browser data removes unsynced local entries.

Membership costs $15 USD per month, plus applicable tax. It renews automatically until canceled. Whop shows your total, billing schedule, and renewal date before payment. Manage or cancel your membership in Whop before the next renewal. Cancellation normally retains access for time already paid; refunds or payment reversals can end access sooner.

If paid access ends, you can sign in to read and export your saved tracker record. New account saves require active access. The free browser preview remains available. Membership does not include AI scanning or Reki iPhone Pro.

Keep your Whop account secure. Do not use the service to access another person's data or interfere with its operation. For access or billing problems, contact admin@rekisupplement.org.

Privacy & your data

In the free preview, supplement names, serving notes, schedules, check-offs, and energy/sleep/mood ratings are saved in this browser's local storage. Preview searches use a bundled list and do not send search terms to an external catalog.

When you sign in with an email link, we store your email address along with server-side session records, subscription/payment references, paid-through dates, and revocation status to identify your account and verify access. Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and work once.

Member tracker records and tour preferences are stored on the Reki Web server and retrieved for the signed-in account. Whop processes checkout; we do not collect card details and there is no Reki password to steal. Tracker content is not sent to Whop for billing verification. Whop handles its own data under the policies linked in its checkout.

Cookies keep you signed in and bind the sign-in attempt to your browser. HTTPS protects requests to the public service. Hosting and proxy systems may process connection details, such as IP addresses and request metadata, for operation and security. This page does not promise anonymous use or zero retention.

Your saved record is retained after membership ends so that you can read or export it. Sign in and use Export my data to download your server record. For corrections, a deletion request, or privacy questions, email admin@rekisupplement.org. Deleting browser data is not a request to delete server records. Cancellation is not a deletion request either.

A browser may still contain earlier preview data after sign-out. Clear site data before handing a shared device to someone else. Avoid entering sensitive information you do not want stored.

Refunds & billing help

Review the refund terms displayed in the Whop checkout before purchasing. This page does not offer an additional guaranteed refund period or override your statutory rights.

For a refund request, use the help or resolution options attached to your purchase in Whop, or email admin@rekisupplement.org with your purchase reference. Do not send payment-card details or passwords.

Canceling future renewals does not by itself refund an earlier payment. A completed refund can revoke paid access. The checkout's terms and applicable law govern eligibility.
